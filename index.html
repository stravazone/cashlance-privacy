Cashlance Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Cashlance ("the app", "we") is a money tracker for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it. In short: your financial data stays on your phone, and we do not collect it.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. There is no account, no login and no Cashlance server. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone, in the app's private on-device storage:

  • Accounts, balances and account categories
  • Transactions, notes, categories and recurring transactions
  • Bookmarks, budget and app settings (such as your default currency and reminder time)
  • The currency of the last receipt you scanned, so the next scan can start from it

This information never leaves your device through the app. On iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in an iCloud or computer backup under Apple's own privacy terms. On Android, the app opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup, so its data is not included in it. If you delete the app, its data is removed from your device.

3. Camera and receipt scanning

If you scan a receipt, the app uses your camera or a photo you choose. The text is read on your phone, using the phone's built-in text recognition on iPhone and an on-device text recognition model bundled with the app on Android. The photo is not saved and is not sent to us or to anyone else. The app asks for camera permission the first time you use this feature, and you can turn it off at any time in your phone's settings.

Location

If a receipt does not say which currency it is in, the app can use your approximate location to guess the currency of the country you are in. The app asks for permission the first time you scan a receipt, and you can say no or turn it off in your phone's settings at any time. Only a rough position is used (within a few kilometers). The country is worked out on your phone from a small list bundled with the app. Your location is not stored, not sent to us, and not sent to any other service.

4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone at the time you choose. It is created and delivered locally. We do not use a push notification server. On Android the app may reschedule the reminder after your phone restarts, which is why it asks to receive the restart signal. You can turn the reminder off in the app or in your phone's settings.

5. Internet connections

The app connects to the internet only to:

  • Get exchange rates. The app requests currency and cryptocurrency rates from public rate services (including a free currency data service and Coinbase's public price data). These requests do not contain your accounts, transactions or personal details.
  • Show brand logos. When you type a note, the app may request a small logo image for a brand name from Google's icon service. The request contains the brand's website address, not your data.

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address and basic technical details. We do not receive or store this information. Each service applies its own privacy policy.

6. Children

Cashlance does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can edit or delete any of your data inside the app, and you can remove all of it by deleting the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding advertising or optional subscriptions, we will update this policy and the date above before the change reaches you. Where required, we will ask for your permission first.
